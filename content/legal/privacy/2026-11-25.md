This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Mitaito Lab collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can have it deleted. It applies to the Mitaito Lab website and to every Mitaito product that uses a Mitaito account (together, the “Services”).

## 1. Who we are

Mitaito Lab is an independent project operated by an individual based in Vietnam. It is not a separate legal entity. In this Policy, “Mitaito”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean that operator.

For any privacy question or request, email **support@mail.mitaito.com**.

## 2. What we collect

We collect only what the Services need to work.

### Your account

One Mitaito account works across every Mitaito product. Accounts are managed by Clerk, an authentication provider. When you sign up or sign in, Clerk processes:

- your email address, and any name or profile image you choose to add;
- if you sign in with a third-party provider, the basic profile that provider shares;
- sign-in and security events, such as when you signed in and verification status; and
- the date and time you accepted our Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy.

Signing up sends your account data to Clerk only. We never see or store your password.

### Data a product creates

When you use a product, that product sends the data it creates for you to our servers and stores it so the product can work for you, for example game history, saved settings, or content you create in the product. Each product stores only the data it needs, linked to your account.

### Technical logs

Our servers keep temporary technical logs, which may include your IP address, browser user-agent, the address requested, timestamps, and error details. We use them only to keep the Services running, fix problems, and protect against abuse, and delete them within **7 days**.

### What we do not collect

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data.

## 3. How we use your data

We use your data to:

- create your account and sign you in;
- provide each product and keep the data it saves for you;
- keep the Services secure and investigate abuse or misuse;
- answer your requests and tell you about important changes; and
- meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Where your data is stored

| Data | Handled by | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account data | Clerk | Clerk's infrastructure, primarily in the United States. See [Clerk's Privacy Policy](https://clerk.com/legal/privacy). |
| Product data and technical logs | Mitaito, on a private cloud we operate ourselves | Singapore |

We do not use any other company to store product data. Because the operator is in Vietnam and the servers are in Singapore and the United States, using the Services involves transferring personal data outside Vietnam. We protect it as described in section 7.

## 5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only:

- with **Clerk**, to run your account;
- when the law requires it, or when it is necessary to protect the safety of users, the public, or the Services.

We do not share your data with anyone else.

If you report a bug, suggest a feature, or propose a partnership through our GitHub issue forms, that content is posted publicly on GitHub and handled under GitHub's own terms. Please do not include personal or confidential details there.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Account data and product data** are kept for as long as your account exists.
- **Technical logs** are deleted within **7 days**.
- **Backups** expire automatically within **7 days**.

When you delete your account (section 8), we delete your account and all product data linked to it from our active systems. Copies that remain in backups expire within 7 days and are not used for anything else in the meantime.

## 7. Security

We protect your data with access controls on our servers, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and by limiting what we collect. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires it.

If you think your account has been compromised, email **support@mail.mitaito.com** right away.

## 8. Your rights

You can:

- **see and correct** your account details in your account settings;
- **delete your account** in your account settings. This deletes your account and all product data linked to it, as described in section 6;
- **ask for a copy** of your data, ask us to correct or restrict it, or withdraw your consent, by emailing **support@mail.mitaito.com**; and
- complain to a data-protection authority, including the authority in Vietnam.

We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request sent by email. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it, but some Services cannot work without the data they need.

## 9. Cookies and browser storage

We use only what the Services need to work:

- Clerk sets cookies that keep you signed in. They are required for your account.
- The Mitaito Lab website stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser.

We do not use cookies for analytics or advertising.

## 10. Children

The Services are not intended for children under **16**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, email **support@mail.mitaito.com** and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

Each version of this Policy is published at this page, kept permanently at a dated address such as `/privacy/2026-11-25`, and stored with its full history in the public Mitaito Lab repository. The version is identified by its effective date.

If we make a material change, we will publish a new version with a new effective date and ask you to accept it before you continue using the Services. The date and time Clerk records for your acceptance, together with these dated versions, shows which version you accepted.

## 12. Contact

Email **support@mail.mitaito.com** for any question about this Policy or your data.
